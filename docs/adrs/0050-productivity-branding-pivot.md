# 0050. Strategic Transition to Productivity Branding

Date: 2026-01-30

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The application was initially developed with a strong emphasis on "Science" and "Symptom Tracking." However, such medical-adjacent terminology often triggers strict regulatory reviews (e.g., FDA-style scrutiny or App Store "Medicine" category requirements) which can be challenging for an independent developer to maintain. Additionally, the core user base is seeking peak performance and focus rather than medical management.

## Decision

We will pivot the application's branding and nomenclature from a "Health/Science" focus to a "Productivity/Strategy" focus.

### Key Nomenclature Changes:
- **ScienceHub** -> **StrategyHub**: Repositioning scientific research as actionable optimization strategies.
- **DailySymptomCheckin** -> **DailyFocusCheckin**: Shifting the focus from "illness/symptoms" to "productivity/focus levels."
- **Clinical/Medical Terms**: Any lingering references to "treatment," "cure," or "symptoms" will be replaced with "protocol," "optimizer," or "state."

### UI/UX Implications:
- The design will lean further into high-performance aesthetics (dark mode, gold accents, precision-focused icons).
- Copywriting will emphasize "Bio-Optimization" and "Flow State" over clinical validity.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Simplified App Store compliance for an individual developer account.
- More attractive positioning for the target audience (ADHD professionals and students).
- Clearer value proposition centered on "Success" and "Output."

**Negative:**
- Some legacy documentation or internal class names may require mapping/refactoring (mitigated by bulk renaming).
- Risk of being perceived as less "rigorous" if the branding is too soft (mitigated by maintaining deep chemist analysis).
