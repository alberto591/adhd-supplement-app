### Full Description (Max 4,000 chars)
**Master Your Focus. Simplify Your Routine.**

NeuroStack is the ultimate supplement and medication tracking companion designed specifically for the ADHD brain. Whether you are managing complex "Focus Stacks" or simple daily vitamins, NeuroStack provides the clarity, consistency, and scientific rigor you need to perform at your best.

Why NeuroStack?
Traditional trackers are cluttered and overwhelming. NeuroStack uses high-contrast, ADHD-friendly design principles to reduce cognitive load and help you stay on track without the "nagging" anxiety or the dreaded "app fatigue."

**🚀 Key Features:**

- **Smart "Nudge" Reminders**: Get gentle, effective anchors for your morning, afternoon, evening, and night routines. We don't just buzz your phone; we help you find your flow.
- **Supplement Intelligence**: Explore a curated database of focus-enhancing supplements. Understand the "why" behind your routine with evidence-based insights, mechanism of action, and timing rationale.
- **Personalized "Focus Stacks"**: Easily organize your supplements and medications into logical daily groups. See exactly what you need to take and when, all at a glance.
- **Safety First**: Our integrated medication intelligence helps you understand how your stacks work together, ensuring you stay informed about your health.
- **ADHD-Friendly Design**: High-contrast visuals, clean layouts, and zero clutter. Finally, a productivity tool that doesn't feel like work.
- **Privacy & Control**: Your health data belongs to you. We use secure encryption and provide easy, transparent tools for data management and deletion.

**Who is this for?**
Whether you have been newly diagnosed or have been managing ADHD for years, NeuroStack is built for anyone looking to optimize their brain through better consistency, education, and routine management.

**⚠️ Medical Disclaimer:**
NeuroStack is an educational and organizational tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses, prescriptions, or treatment advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional (Physician or Psychiatrist) before starting any new supplement or medication regimen, especially if you have an existing medical condition or are taking other prescriptions.
